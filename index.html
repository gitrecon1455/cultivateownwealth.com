⚠ Subdomain Takeover

This Subdomain Is Controlled By An External Party

This page demonstrates the impact of a Subdomain Takeover vulnerability. The attacker does not own the parent domain but is able to host arbitrary content on this trusted subdomain.

Impact:

As an attacker, I can host any content on this subdomain even though I never purchased or owned the domain.

This may allow:

  • Phishing Pages
  • Fake Login Portals
  • Malware Distribution
  • Brand Impersonation
  • Social Engineering Attacks
  • Hosting Arbitrary Content
